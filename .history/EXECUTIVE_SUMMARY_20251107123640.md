# Flolabs.international Migration - Executive Summary

## 📊 Project Overview

I've thoroughly analyzed your current WordPress website at flolabs.international and created a comprehensive migration plan to move to a modern, maintainable stack that will save you money and make it easy for non-coders to update content.

## 🔍 What I Found

### Current Site Structure
- **Homepage**: Hero section, 4 projects showcase, 4 verticals, benefits grid, methodology section
- **Internships Page**: 35+ active position listings with search functionality

### Technical Issues Identified

- Performance concerns
- Higher maintenance costs

## 💡 Recommended Solution

### Technology Stack

**Frontend**: Next.js 14 (React framework)
- ✅ Excellent SEO (crucial for your business)
- ✅ Lightning-fast page loads
- ✅ Modern, maintainable code
- ✅ Free hosting on Vercel

**CMS**: Sanity.io (NOT Sentry - that's for error tracking)
- ✅ **FREE Tier** - 3 users, 10k documents, 5GB assets
- ✅ **Beautiful, intuitive Studio** - Non-coders will love it
- ✅ Cloud-hosted - No server management needed
- ✅ Real-time collaboration
- ✅ Easy to manage internships, blogs, projects
- ✅ Excellent Next.js integration

**Note**: Originally recommended Payload CMS, but they're not accepting new cloud signups currently. Sanity is the best free alternative.

**Database**: PostgreSQL
- ✅ Industry standard, reliable
- ✅ Free tier available (Vercel Postgres or Supabase)

**Hosting**: Vercel
- ✅ **FREE for personal/small business use**
- ✅ Automatic deployments from GitHub
- ✅ Global CDN for fast worldwide access
- ✅ Built-in SSL certificates
